Motorboer

A fully functional electric motorised wheelbarrow, designed and built from scratch over six weeks at Maskinmesterskolen København.

The mechanical work spanned TIG and MIG welding of a square steel frame and tipping bed, and a complete custom gearbox with four machined spur gears and two belt drives, reducing motor speed from 3000 RPM down to around 190 RPM at the wheel axle, giving a top speed of roughly 23 km/h. Gear wheels, axles, hubs, and brass bushings were all turned and milled in-house to technical drawings.

The pneumatic system featured a custom-welded pressure tank, an onboard 12V compressor, and a 5/3 joystick-controlled valve operating a tipping cylinder capable of lifting over 100 kg and holding position mid-stroke. Electrical work covered forward/reverse contactor logic, a motor controller, emergency stop, and key switch, all wired and routed internally through the frame.

The project was a study in how fabrication, machining, pneumatics, and electrical control can be brought together into a single working machine.
